What Is It?

A walking challenge that turns your steps into a mission.

Mission: On The Move is a 4-week team-based walking challenge powered by the A Step Ahead™ app. Your everyday steps become the fuel for an interactive adventure where teams race through a story together.

It's not just a fitness tracker — it's a game. You and your teammates use real-world steps to move through the story, while friendly competition keeps everyone motivated to the finish.

Best of all, every participant becomes part of the Angels Wings Transport community — connected to the veterans, families, and animals your effort helps support.

Who Can Join

Everyone's welcome on the mission

Whether you come as an organized team or a single walker, there's a place for you in the challenge.

🎖️

Veterans & Posts

American Legion and VFW posts can field their own teams. AWT was founded by a veteran, for veterans — this is a mission worth marching for.

🐾

The Pet Community

Veterinary clinics, groomers, and pet stores can compete as teams and rally their clients behind the animals AWT flies to safety.

🤝

Individuals & Supporters

No team? No problem. Join as an individual and we'll place you on a community team alongside fellow AWT supporters.

Why It Matters
"Changing a life with every flight."

Angels Wings Transport is an all-volunteer 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Using a network of volunteer pilots flying their own aircraft, we provide free air transport for wounded warriors and military families, rescue animals escaping kill shelters, and medical patients who can't afford the journey to care.

When you walk in Mission: On The Move, you're not just getting healthier — you're growing the community that keeps these flights in the air.
